How mail-in repair works

  1. Submit the form below with your device and issue. We'll follow up with a price estimate and confirm before you ship anything.

Mail-In Repair Terms & Conditions

Last updated: September 2026

60-Day Guarantee

All mail-in repairs performed by 559 Mobile Repairs are covered by a 60-day guarantee from the date the repair is completed. If the specific issue that was repaired recurs within that window due to a defect in parts or workmanship, we will repair or replace the affected part at no additional cost.

What's covered

  • Failure of the specific part we installed (e.g. a replaced screen or charging port)
  • Workmanship issues directly related to the repair performed

What's not covered

  • New physical damage after the repair (drops, cracks, or impact unrelated to the original repair)
  • Liquid or water damage occurring after the device is returned
  • Damage caused by unauthorized repairs or modifications made by a third party after our repair
  • Normal wear and tear, or cosmetic issues that don't affect function
  • Issues unrelated to the component we repaired

Shipping & device responsibility

  • Inbound shipping: You are responsible for your device until it arrives at our shop. We strongly recommend a trackable, insured shipping method. We are not responsible for devices lost or damaged in transit to us.
  • Outbound shipping: We ship your repaired device back using a trackable method to the return address you provide. Please double-check your return address for accuracy before submitting.
  • Packaging: Please pack your device securely (padded box, avoid loose packaging) to prevent shipping damage.

Data & backups

You are responsible for backing up your data before sending in your device. While we take care during every repair, 559 Mobile Repairs is not responsible for data loss that may occur during the repair process. We recommend backing up your device before shipping it to us.

Diagnosis & pricing

We provide a price estimate before beginning any repair, based on the information you submit and, where needed, a physical inspection once your device arrives. If the actual issue differs from what was described and changes the cost, we'll contact you before proceeding.

Turnaround time

Most mail-in repairs are completed within 2–3 business days of your device arriving at our shop, not including shipping time to and from you. Turnaround may vary based on parts availability.
